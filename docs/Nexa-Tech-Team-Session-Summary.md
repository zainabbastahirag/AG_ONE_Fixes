# Nexa — Tech Team Alignment Session

**Purpose:** Align on Nexa’s current state, priorities, and the inputs needed from Product to support continuous improvement within the AG ONE ecosystem.

**Reference material:** UAT findings — *UAT - 28th June 2026.xlsx* (Nexa tab)

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Current capabilities and product vision
- Review what Nexa can do today
- Assess alignment with the intended product vision and AG ONE strategy

### 2. UAT review (28 June 2026)
- Walk through items listed in the **Nexa** tab of the UAT spreadsheet
- Confirm status, severity, and ownership for open items

### 3. Hybrid knowledge model
- How Nexa combines **knowledge-base (KB) content** with **dynamic data sources**
- Gaps, risks, and opportunities in the current approach

### 4. Lead conversion and user journeys
- Navigation guidance
- Lead generation and sign-up flows
- Product discovery
- Purchase-related journeys and handoffs

### 5. Response formats, behavior, and UX
- Tone, structure, and presentation of responses
- Interaction patterns and consistency across touchpoints
- Overall end-user experience quality

### 6. Troubleshooting, support, and guidance
- In-conversation troubleshooting and how-to support
- Support ticket submission and escalation paths
- Boundaries between self-serve guidance and human support
